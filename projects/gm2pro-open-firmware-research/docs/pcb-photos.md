# PCB Photos

Store curated, publishable photos in `hardware/photos/`. Keep raw originals in `hardware/photos/originals/` if they include private labels or are too large for git.

## Required Shots

- Earbud PCB top and bottom, flat and in focus.
- SoC closeup with readable marking.
- Flash/PMIC closeups.
- Test pad field closeups with temporary labels.
- Antenna and RF matching network.
- Case PCB top and bottom.
- Pogo pins and case-to-bud contact geometry.

## Annotation Convention

- Use `TP1`, `TP2`, etc. for unknown pads.
- Rename only after confirmed: `GND`, `VBAT`, `VCC_1V8`, `UART_TX`, `UART_RX`, `SWDIO`, `SWCLK`, `RST`, `SPI_MISO`, `SPI_MOSI`, `SPI_CLK`, `SPI_CS`.
- Every annotation should cite the measurement or trace that supports it.
